=== LENH DEMO (Windows PowerShell: dung curl.exe thay cho curl) ===

--- Kiem tra 2 service ---
curl http://localhost:8081/products/1
curl http://localhost:8080/orders/1

--- Theo doi trang thai mach (mo san o tab khac) ---
curl http://localhost:8080/actuator/circuitbreakers
curl http://localhost:8080/actuator/circuitbreakerevents

=== DEMO CHINH (Nguoi 5) ===
1. Ca 2 service dang chay:      curl http://localhost:8080/orders/1      -> du lieu that, CLOSED
2. Tat product-service (Ctrl+C), goi lai 5 lan:
                                curl http://localhost:8080/orders/1      -> fallback (RetryableException)
3. Goi tiep:                    curl http://localhost:8080/orders/1      -> fallback (CallNotPermittedException), OPEN
4. Bat lai product-service, cho hon 10 giay, goi 2 lan:
                                curl http://localhost:8080/orders/1      -> HALF_OPEN -> CLOSED

=== DEMO 2 LOI (Nguoi 6) - tat product-service truoc ===
curl http://localhost:8080/orders/1                 -> fallback (dung)
curl http://localhost:8080/orders/1/noi-bo          -> loi 500, actuator khong dem them
curl http://localhost:8080/orders/1/fallback-sai    -> loi 500, xem canh bao trong log order-service
